How Volant Mall Works

A marketplace where Ghanaian sellers open their own stores, and buyers shop from all of them in one place.

1

Browse the mall

Shop across all sellers, or visit a seller's own store. Filter by category — beauty, fashion, electronics and more.

2

Choose pickup or delivery

Pick up at the seller's location (free) or have it delivered to your address for the seller's delivery fee.

3

Pay with MoMo or card

Checkout securely with Paystack — MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash or debit card, in Ghana cedis.

4

Track your order

See your order status in My Orders. The seller marks it delivered once it reaches you.

1

Open your store

Sign in, name your store, add your city and bio. Attach your own logo and banner to make it yours.

2

Set up payouts

Add your MTN MoMo number or bank account. Paystack settles your sales straight to you.

3

List your products

Add up to 4 photos, price in GHS, stock, and choose pickup and/or delivery per item.

4

Fulfil and earn

Get notified of orders in your dashboard, pack & ship (or meet buyers), and watch earnings grow.

Seller commission: 10% of each sale goes to Volant to keep the platform running — Paystack payout fees are covered from that share. Buyers pay no extra fees.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I pay?
Checkout happens inside the cart with Paystack. You can pay with MTN Mobile Money, Telecel Cash, or any bank card (Visa/Mastercard). All prices are in Ghana cedis (GHS).
Can I get delivery anywhere in Ghana?
Every product shows its delivery options. Sellers choose whether they offer delivery, which regions they cover, and the delivery fee. If a seller offers pickup, you can also collect the item in person at their location — pickup is free.
When will my order arrive?
Delivery times depend on the seller and distance. Once you order, the seller prepares your item and updates the order to "Delivered / Completed". You can track it anytime under My Orders.
How do sellers get paid?
After a sale, Paystack automatically puts 90% of the money into the seller's registered MTN MoMo or bank account (settlement usually within a few business days). Volant keeps a 10% commission.
What if I have a problem with an order?
Contact the seller first — their phone number is on your order. If you can't resolve it, reach Volant support through the Contact page and include your order reference.
Can I review a product?
Yes! After you've bought a product, you can leave a 1–5 star rating and a short review on its product page. Reviews appear on the product and the seller's storefront.
How do I become a seller?
Click "Sell" at the top, sign in, set up your store and payout details, then list your first product. It takes about 5 minutes and is free to start.
What can I sell on Volant Mall?
Physical goods you can fulfil in Ghana — pomade and beauty products, clothes, shoes, electronics, home items, food products and more. Counterfeit or prohibited items are not allowed.
