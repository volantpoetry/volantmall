Refunds & Returns

What happens if something isn't right with your order.

1. Talk to the seller first

Every order on Volant Mall is fulfilled by an independent seller. If an item is not what you ordered, damaged, or late, contact the seller directly from your order confirmation (their phone number and your order details are on the My Orders page).

2. For delivery problems

If a delivery order was not delivered or arrived damaged, tell the seller within 48 hours of receipt. Sellers can offer a replacement, a partial refund, or a full refund for confirmed problems with the item itself.

3. Refunds happen through Paystack

Refunds are processed back to the payment method used at checkout (Mobile Money or card). Once the seller approves a refund, please allow 5–10 working days for it to reflect depending on your bank or mobile money operator.

4. Seller not responding?

If you can't reach an agreement with the seller within 7 days, contact our team via Contact Us with your order reference. Volant Mall can step in, review the order, and, where the seller is at fault, issue the refund from the seller's settlement.

5. Digital products

Digital downloads are non-refundable once delivered, unless the file is unusable.

Your payment is always protected on Paystack. We never see or store your card or MoMo details.
